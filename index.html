The Cosmic Framework of Hinduism

Hinduism is built upon deep cosmic order, cyclical time scales (Yugas), and the manifestation of supreme consciousness through distinct divine entities. Within this system, Garuda serves as a sacred, powerful mount, navigating the lineages of cosmic manifestation and cosmic order.
